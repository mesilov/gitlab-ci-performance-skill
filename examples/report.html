How this is counted

  • Pipeline run time is the time when at least one job was running. Retries count; runner waits and pauses before manual jobs do not.
  • Medians use successful runs only. Failed runs are drawn but do not move the median.
  • Change compares the median of the newest successful runs with the earlier ones. It is marked only when it is above 20% and 30 s.
  • Steps come from the log of one run, linked next to them. They are an example, not an average.
